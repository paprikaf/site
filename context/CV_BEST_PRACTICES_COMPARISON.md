## 📚 Industry Best Practices (2025)

Based on research from leading career resources:

### ✅ Structure & Content
1. **Length:** 1-2 pages
2. **Professional Summary:** Concise statement with key skills and objectives
3. **Reverse Chronological Order:** Most recent experience first
4. **Quantifiable Achievements:** Specific metrics (e.g., "Increased sales by 15%")
5. **Bullet Points:** Clear, digestible format
6. **Tailored Content:** Customize for each job application
7. **ATS-Friendly:** Include keywords from job descriptions

### ✅ Formatting
1. **Professional Fonts:** Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman (10-12pt)
2. **Margins:** 0.5-1 inch
3. **Consistent Formatting:** Uniform throughout
4. **Clear Section Headings:** Easy to navigate
5. **PDF Format:** Preserve formatting

### ✅ Language & Tone
1. **Action Verbs:** Start bullets with strong verbs (Built, Managed, Advised)
2. **Clear & Concise:** No unnecessary words
3. **Professional Tone:** Avoid casual language
4. **Avoid Jargon:** Use industry terms judiciously

### ❌ What to Avoid
1. **Personal Details:** Age, gender, marital status, religion
2. **Photos:** Unless specifically requested
3. **Informal Email:** Use professional email address
4. **Spelling/Grammar Errors:** Proofread thoroughly
5. **Generic Content:** Tailor to each application
