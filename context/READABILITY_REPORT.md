## 🎯 Readability Guidelines

### Fog Index Ranges
- **0-6:** Very Easy (5th-6th grade)
- **7-9:** Easy (7th-9th grade)
- **10-12:** Fairly Easy (High school)
- **13-16:** Difficult (College level)
- **17-20:** Hard to Read (Professional/specialized knowledge)
- **21+:** Very Hard to Read (Graduate level)

### Target for Professional Resume
**Ideal Range:** 14-18  
**Why:** Professional audiences expect technical terminology and industry jargon. Too simple = unprofessional. Too complex = hard to skim.

## 📝 Writing Principles Applied

### 1. **One Idea Per Sentence**
Break complex sentences into multiple simple ones.

### 2. **Active Voice**
Use active verbs: "Built", "Managed", "Advised", "Scaled"

### 3. **Concrete Numbers**
Keep quantifiable achievements: 500+ users, 40%, $300K+

### 4. **Remove Fluff**
- ❌ "exceptional demonstration of company values"
- ✅ Just state the award

### 5. **Professional Tone**
- ❌ "no designer or PM needed" (defensive)
- ✅ Focus on what you built, not what you don't need
