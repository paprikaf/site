## 💡 Tips for Answering

### Be Specific
- ❌ "I worked on many projects"
- ✅ "I built the VMware Electron app serving 10K+ users"

### Include Stories
- Numbers are great, but stories are memorable
- "The moment I realized..." type anecdotes
- Challenges you faced and how you overcame them

### Think RAG Context
- The more detail, the better the chat agent will be
- Include context that helps answer "why" and "how"
- Don't worry about being too verbose

### Be Authentic
- Genuine experiences > polished advice
- Include failures and lessons learned
- Your unique perspective is valuable
